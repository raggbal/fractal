Notes list (.fractal)

Choose the .fractal file Fractal uses. The clipper reads your notes from it every time, so notes you add in Fractal show up here by themselves. The default is ~/.fractal/default.fractal (on a Mac, press ⌘⇧G in the dialog and type ~/.fractal).
