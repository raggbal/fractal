# Beta partner FAQ (draft)

## Q: How long does the private beta run?

From **July 1 to mid-September 2026**. GA launch is planned for September 15.

## Q: What does the beta include?

| Included | Not included |
|----------|--------------|
| Full product access, unlimited seats | SLA guarantees |
| Direct Slack channel with the team | SOC 2 report (in progress) |
| Weekly office hours | Data residency options |
| Locked-in launch pricing (-30%) | |

## Q: How is beta pricing calculated?

Beta partners get 30% off the launch price, locked for 12 months:

```math
P_{beta} = P_{launch} \times 0.7 = \$49 \times 0.7 = \$34.30 \text{ /mo (Team tier)}
```

## Q: What happens to our data after the beta?

Nothing changes — beta accounts convert in place. You can export everything (JSON + CSV) at any time from **Settings → Export**.

## Q: How do we report bugs?

Slack channel first for anything urgent. Everything else via the in-app feedback widget — it attaches logs automatically.
